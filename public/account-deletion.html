Delete your Kharch Baant account

Google Play and privacy law require a way to delete your account. You can do it in the app or request it by email.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Kharch Baant and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

This removes your login from your profile, clears your name, email, and photo, and signs you out. Expense history in shared groups is kept for other members, with your name shown as “Deleted user”.

If you cannot open the app

Email the address on the Play Store listing from the same email you used to sign up. Write “Delete my Kharch Baant account” and we will process the request.
